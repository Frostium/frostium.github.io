Time and again, the world comes of age.
And as time continues, many wars rage.

But eternity waits, strong, real, and true.
Friendships will stay, and our memories too.

So be kind, and loving, and thoughtful you should.
Since this earth is bad, something is good.

Some new thing is coming, you just have to wait.
Then ther'll be no more pain and be no more hate.

When Jesus comes, then we'll be home.
But for now let's be soldiers, not at all alone.
