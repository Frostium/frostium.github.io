Deep in the heart of the snicketies home, 
lived a black and an evil pigamee drone.
With two horns on his head and a ring in his nose, 
this was no ordinary pigamee drone.
He stomped through the snicketies green, fair, farmed land,
and broke all their houses, black hammer in hand.

But which champion fighter will beat up this drone? 
Blacken and beaten all his tidy made bones?

A tall handsome man stood up from the crowd, 
watching and waiting for the drone to come down, 
He picked up an arrow and drew it to string. The man let loose bow and, wow! Watch that thing, watch that thing go!

The crowd huddled round and let loose a shout, 
"Jibberdy John, what were we scared about? 
We knew you would come, we knew you would fight; 
and now look we finally see the new light."

But while the crowd shouted and screamed with fresh glee the pigamee drone looked down,
upon the snickety's arrow as it fell short,
and hit the drones sandle strap with a "snort."

He stomped upon Jibberdy John ,
and smiled as he looked at the dawn;
setting backwards because of him, 
and then came a champion called floppedy Jim.

That Jim just smiled and took out a spear, 
and ran right toward him without letting loose a tear.

The pigamee drone was just way too big. 
He picked up Floppedy Jim and threw him just like a fig.

Then a young boy came up from the crowd 
and took out a stone and said with a shout, 
"How dare you threaten my friends;
my people, it just doesn't make ends?"

With that, lamnammer the boy took out his stone;
He threw it so hard it beat Johnny tremone.

Lamnammer hit that beast so hard he fell to the ground;
He hit it so hard he made a big pound.

All the people rejoiced,
but said with a loud voice,
"Is it really dead?"
And that is what they actually said.

So the boy Lamnammer took up the beats hammer and cut off his head in fron of king Tanner.

Then the boy was a hero for killing the beast,
and all the snicketies had a roast beast feast.

The end.


*Recovered from a box in my closet. Estimated written in 2017 when I was 10.*
